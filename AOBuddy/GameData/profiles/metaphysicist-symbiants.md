# Meta-Physicist - Best-in-Slot Symbiants (endgame)

A top MP fills all 13 implant slots with CONTROL-Unit symbiants - the nano/pet-caster unit (the Control line is profession-locked to Engineer, Trader, Bureaucrat and Meta-Physicist; verified stat-60 lock {3,7,8,12} in items.ocp, and both the ao-universe and Funcom guides put MP in the Control unit). Control symbiants stack the six nano schools (Matter Creation, Time & Space, Bio Met, Matter Met, Psycho Mod, Sensory Impr) plus Nano Pool, Max Nano, Nano Init, Treatment and Nano Resist - exactly the caster/pet stats an MP wants, which is why symbiants outclass cluster implants here. Best-in-slot at endgame is the Xan Control-Unit ALPHA in every slot (highest bonuses of the line: e.g. all six nano schools +144 and +574 Max Nano on the Brain/Head Alpha). Progression to reach them: wear the Xan Control Beta first (lower reqs), then swap each slot to the crafted Alpha once Treatment/Int/Psychic/level allow.

## Which symbiant line for an MP?

- **Recommended unit: Control** - Control is the caster/pet unit shared by MP/Crat/Engi/Trader; it carries the nano-school + nano-pool + nano-init bonuses an MP lives on. Verified: every Control symbiant's ToWear action profession-locks to stat 60 EqualTo {3 Engineer, 7 Trader, 8 Bureaucrat, 12 Meta-Physicist} (items.ocp).
- MP-usable units per ao-universe: Control, Extermination, Support. The ao-universe symbiant guide lists Control, Extermination and Support as MP-usable; Control is the nano-caster pick used here. Extermination/Support trade nano bonuses for other stats and are not best-in-slot for a caster MP.

## Xan Alpha crafting path

Xan Alpha symbiants are the most advanced symbiants in AO. They cannot be looted - each is crafted by the player from two QL300 components and is NODROP / cannot be reverted once assembled.

**Recipe:** Combine (drag + shift-click, or the tradeskill window) a Xan <slot> Symbiant, Control Unit Beta with an Intelligent <slot> Symbiant, Control Unit Aban QL300 of the SAME slot and unit. No tradeskill tool or nano is required.

**Components:**
- *Xan Beta:* Xan Control-Unit Beta for the slot. Betas drop from the 3 Legacy-of-the-Xan instances; the Ear and Thigh Betas instead come from separate quests (Arid Rift / Neretva) and are NODROP.
- *Intelligent QL300 (SL base):* Intelligent Control-Unit (Aban) QL300 for the slot - the top-QL Shadowlands pocket-boss symbiant. The QL300 Intelligent tier drops from The Night Heart on the Pandemonium islands (real or instanced). Tradeable (YESDROP).

**To assemble:** Treatment 2088; Three abilities: two fixed by unit + one variable by slot. For Control the two fixed are Intelligence + Psychic. (Skill needed to perform the combine (ao-universe Alpha-symbiant tradeskill guide).)

**Clicky trick:** Xan Beta Ear and Thigh are clickable: right-click cycles their unit type. Buy the cheapest Intelligent Ear/Thigh of any unit, cycle the Beta to match, combine, then cycle the resulting Alpha back to Control. (Funcom guide.)

**Why Alpha is best-in-slot:** Per slot the Alpha beats both components. Head example: Beta nano-schools +130 / Max Nano +519; Intelligent (SL QL300) +137 / +547; Alpha +144 / +574. Alpha is a straight upgrade of the QL300 SL symbiant, with the same equip reqs.
